### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment at FCJ is very friendly and open. Members are always willing to help whenever I encounter difficulties, even outside working hours. The workspace is tidy and comfortable, helping me focus better. However, I think it would be nice to have more social gatherings or team bonding activities to strengthen relationships among interns and mentors.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentor provides very detailed guidance, explains clearly when I don't understand, and always encourages me to ask questions. The admin team supports administrative tasks, provides necessary documents, and creates favorable conditions for me to work effectively. I especially appreciate that the mentor allows me to try and solve problems myself instead of just giving the answer directly.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The internship content is highly relevant to my major in Artificial Intelligence, while also introducing me to many practical areas of cloud computing I had not encountered at university. This allowed me to both reinforce my foundational knowledge and develop new hands-on skills with AWS services.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
During the internship, I had the opportunity to gain practical knowledge of AWS cloud services, system design thinking, technical report writing, and professional communication. Attending workshops and technical events also broadened my perspective on cloud computing and AI trends, and helped me better plan my career path.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The culture is very positive: everyone respects each other, works seriously but still keeps things enjoyable. Even as an intern, I always felt like a real part of the team and that my opinions were welcomed during both learning activities and technical discussions.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The program provides good conditions for interns in terms of learning resources, participating in activities, and accessing technical materials. Being able to attend internal sharing sessions, workshops, and technology events is a major advantage that makes the internship practical rather than purely theoretical.

---

### Additional Questions

- **What I was most satisfied with during the internship:**  
  What I appreciated most was the opportunity to gain hands-on experience with real AWS services in a structured yet open and supportive environment, and to attend technical events that gave me exposure to real-world cloud and AI applications.

- **What I think the program could improve for future interns:**  
  I think the program could benefit from more networking and experience-sharing sessions between different intern cohorts, so that participants can connect and learn from each other more effectively.

- **Would I recommend this internship to my friends? Why?**  
  Yes, absolutely. This program offers a genuinely valuable environment for learning cloud technologies, building professional skills, and gaining practical experience that is difficult to find elsewhere as a student.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

- It would be helpful to have more knowledge-sharing or mentoring sessions between interns and seniors to strengthen connections within the program.
- Adding more hands-on group projects would give interns more opportunities to apply what they learn in a collaborative setting.
- I would be happy to continue participating in similar programs in the future to deepen my knowledge and further develop my professional skills.
- **Other comments:**  
  Overall, I consider this internship to be highly valuable. It helped me gain practical knowledge of AWS, develop a clearer sense of direction for my career in cloud computing, and grow both technically and professionally.
